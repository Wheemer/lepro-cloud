## 🎉 Lepro Cloud v0.2.1

This release corrects native light controls, strengthens E1-30 recognition and improves shared-device favourites.

### Highlights

- 💡 Recovers E1-30 colour and white controls from verified product IDs when model metadata is missing or incorrect, and prevents conflicting plug metadata from creating an on/off-only switch.
- 🎨 Corrects native colour, white, brightness and pattern-length handling across supported RGBIC product families. Adjusting brightness preserves the active pattern.
- ⭐ Improves saved-effect selection and brightness handling, including multipart patterns and app-supplied effect formats.
- 👥 Loads favourites in the correct family context for shared devices and preserves accessible favourites when another family cannot be queried.
- 📡 Accepts valid numeric discovery and MQTT values, improves command-response handling, and recognises additional MQTT-capable transport types.
- 🏷️ Preserves known product capabilities during incomplete discovery responses and removes obsolete primary entities only after their replacement is registered.
- 🛠️ Updates Ruff to 0.17.4 and GitHub CodeQL actions, and adds compatibility checks with Paho MQTT 2.2.0.

Requires Home Assistant 2026.4.0 or newer. No account reconfiguration is required.
